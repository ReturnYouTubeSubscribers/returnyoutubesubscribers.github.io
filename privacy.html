Return YouTube Subscribers

Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026. Applies to the browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Opera, Firefox, Safari) and the userscript.

The short version

The extension sends one thing: the public ID of the YouTube channel you're looking at, to NextCounts or Mixerno.space, to get its subscriber count. We don't collect, store, sell or share anything else, and there's no account.

What is sent, and where

  • To NextCounts (api.nextcounts.com, api-v2.nextcounts.com): about once an hour the extension downloads the list of channels in NextCounts' Unabbreviated Project, which share their real count from YouTube Studio. When you view one of those channels, its channel ID is sent to NextCounts to get that real count. See nextcounts.com for how they handle requests.
  • To backend.mixerno.space: for every other channel (or when NextCounts is unavailable), the channel ID (for example UCx7…) of the channel page, video or search result on screen. Your browser makes this request directly, so Mixerno.space also receives the standard information any website receives, such as your IP address. See Mixerno.space for how they handle requests.
  • To youtube.com: on search result pages the extension loads channel pages from YouTube to find their IDs. These are ordinary requests to YouTube, made with your existing YouTube session.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reports or advertising IDs.
  • Your browsing history is never sent anywhere beyond the lookups above.
  • No personal information, YouTube account details or cookies are read or sent by the extension.
  • On your device only, the extension keeps your number-format setting and the counts of channels you have viewed (up to 240 readings per channel), which power growth rates, milestone projections and the toolbar popup, plus the hourly list of Unabbreviated Project channels. This never leaves your browser; removing the extension deletes it.

This website

ryts.mgcounts.com has no ads. It shows Disqus comments and uses Google Fonts; those services may set cookies and receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. The trailer is embedded from youtube-nocookie.com and only loads when you press play. None of this applies to the extension itself.
